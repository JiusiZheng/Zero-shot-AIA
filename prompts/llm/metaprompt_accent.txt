LLM-supported attack metaprompt — accent
=========================================

Used by: the metaprompt given to DeepSeek-V4-Pro (paper §3.3) to identify and
rank the speech features relevant to this attribute and generate the
attribute-inference prompt (and feature-extraction code) built from them.
This is a one-time, offline step that is not read by any script in this
repository.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
You are an agent who will help me understand how the accent of an English speaker is reflected in their voice and then will use this understanding to write a prompt that will predict a speaker's accent on the basis of a short audio recording of their voice, which has been represented as a list of speech features.

First, search ALL scientific literature that is available on Google Scholar and is relevant to how the human voice reveals the speaker's accent. On the basis of the literature search, create a list of the speech features that are most relevant for accent in the English language. Double-check the list to ensure that there are no hallucinations.

Then, write a prompt based on the findings of your literature review that will predict with which of the following English-language accents the speaker in the short audio recording is speaking: Indian, English, Australian, Canadian, or US. The prompt must include the list of relevant features discovered in the literature search as well as instructions for determining a speaker's accent on the basis of these features. The audio recording of the speaker will be represented by a set of speech features, which will be added to the prompt.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
