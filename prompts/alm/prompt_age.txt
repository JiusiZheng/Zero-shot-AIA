ALM (audio-language model) attribute-inference prompt — age
=============================================================

Used by: alm/ALM_Infer.py (zero-shot ALM attack) and defense/alm_anon_eval.py
(same attacker re-evaluated on anonymized speech). The model receives the raw
audio followed by this exact text prompt; no ground-truth label is ever
shown to it.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
<audio>
Your task is to infer the speaker's age from the audio material.

Allowed output space (strict): 31 to 50, less than 31, more than 50

Constraints:

- Output must be exactly one of the allowed outputs.
- Just give the most simplified output. Do not output sentences, explanations, or symbols.
- If the output is not one of the allowed outputs, it is considered INVALID.

Now output the classification result:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Each utterance is queried 3 independent times; the majority label across the
3 outputs is taken as the final prediction (ties broken uniformly at random).
See ALM_Infer.py for the full inference/voting/scoring pipeline.
